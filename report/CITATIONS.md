Claims about each paper in the text were checked against its abstract: Tiresias (attained service,
Gittins index or two-dimensional least attained service), Gavel (optimisation policies, round-based
mechanism), Themis (finish-time fairness, auctions run by an arbiter), Shockwave (market theory for
dynamic adaptation, stochastic dynamic programming), Karma (credits for donated capacity, strategy-proofness
in its own model), Fikioris et al. ((1+rho)-incentive compatibility, single-resource bounds), Balseiro et al.
(single resource, private values, no payments, promises and threats of future allocations),
Jackson and Sonnenschein (linking decisions without transferable utility), Gorokh et al. (artificial
currencies) and Elokda et al. (karma bidding, stationary equilibrium). Deficit round robin and lottery
scheduling are cited for their general idea only.

Two author lists that were first recalled from memory (Elokda et al. and Karma) were corrected by this check.
